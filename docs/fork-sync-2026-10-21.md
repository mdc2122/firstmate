## Merge attempts and what they proved

1. **Cherry-pick replay** of the 96 non-merge fork commits onto `upstream/main`:
   **92 conflicts, 4 clean**. The fork commits form a chain: later commits edit
   files created by earlier ones, so replay cannot be the strategy.
2. **Three-way merge** `origin/main` into `fork/main`: **100 conflicted files**
   (37 `bin/`, 30 `tests/`, 14 `docs/`, remainder skills, extensions, workflows).
3. **Three-way merge with `-X ours`**: merges cleanly but yields a skewed tree.
   `tests/fm-watch-triage.test.sh` fails with
   `bin/fm-classify-lib.sh: line 780: unstamped: unbound variable`, and
   `bin/fm-lint.sh --partition 1of2` exits 2 with `roots[@]: unbound variable`.
   Upstream rewrote callers in non-conflicting hunks while the fork's callee
   stayed. **Do not merge blindly and do not use `-X ours`.**
