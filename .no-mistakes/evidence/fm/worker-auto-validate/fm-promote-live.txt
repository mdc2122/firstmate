### Scenario 4: promoted scout -> no-mistakes worker (default)
$ fm-promote.sh prom-a1 --mode no-mistakes --yolo off
rc=0
--- delivered message, Definition of done head:
# Definition of done
Delivery contract: mode=no-mistakes
The task is complete only when committed on your branch.
When you believe it is complete, append `working: committed {short sha}; starting validation` to the status file and immediately run /no-mistakes yourself to validate and ship a PR; do not stop or wait for firstmate in between.

You drive no-mistakes by responding to its gates, not by implementing fixes.

### Scenario 5: promoted with --pause-before-validate
$ fm-promote.sh prom-a2 --mode no-mistakes --yolo off --pause-before-validate
rc=0
--- delivered message, Definition of done head:
# Definition of done
Delivery contract: mode=no-mistakes
The task is complete only when committed on your branch.
When you believe it is complete, append `done: {summary}` to the status file and stop.
This task pauses before validating: firstmate reviews the commit first and will then instruct you to run /no-mistakes to validate and ship a PR.
